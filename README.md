## 1. Game Overview

A 2D pixel steampunk action platformer where the player runs, jumps, fights, and moves through dangerous mechanical stages using a teleporting kunai as the main gimmick.

The game is set in a magic-and-machine city with giant gears, pipes, rooftops, flying whales, and scrap-metal buildings, with an Arcane / Avatar-style vibe.

The goal is to clear a short series of rooms by surviving enemies, hazards, and platforming challenges.

## 2. Genre and Player Fantasy

The genre is a 2D platformer mixed with action combat and movement-based gameplay. The focus is not just jumping, but using movement creatively.

The player fantasy is being a fast steampunk ninja / fighter who throws a kunai, teleports across the level, dodges attacks, and fights through mechanical enemies.

The game and combat should feel satisfying.

## 3. Core Gameplay Loop

The player enters a room, reads the platforms, enemies, and hazards, then figures out how to use jumping, kunai throws, and teleporting to get through safely.

The loop is:

Move forward → jump → throw kunai → teleport → fight enemy → avoid hazard → reach checkpoint → enter the next room.

Each room should add new components and art to build the world.

## 4. Player Mechanics

The player can run and jump with responsive controls, because the game only works if the basic movement feels clean.

The player can throw a kunai forward or toward an aimed direction, then teleport to it. This is used for crossing gaps, reaching platforms, dodging, and repositioning.

The player can use a basic melee attack, take damage from enemies or hazards, and respawn after dying.

## 5. Kunai Teleport System

The kunai is the main mechanic, so the levels should constantly give the player reasons to throw it, teleport to it, and chain that with normal platforming.

The kunai should feel supercharged and magical / mechanical, with a glowing trail, sparks, steam burst, or blue energy so it visually stands out.

The kunai should feel versatile.

## 6. Combat and Enemies

Combat should be simple and satisfying, with a basic melee attack and enemies that create pressure without needing a huge combo system.

Enemy type one can be a small walking robot / guard that patrols platforms and forces the player to attack or teleport around it.

Enemy type two can be a ranged robot / turret that shoots projectiles, making the player use the kunai to dodge, close distance, or move behind cover.

## 7. Level Design and Hazards

The game should have around 3 main rooms / stages:

1. Room one teaches movement.
2. Room two combines enemies and hazards.
3. Room three acts as the final challenge.

Hazards should match the world, such as spinning gears, steam vents, hot pipes, exposed electricity, pits, moving platforms, and crushing machinery.

The levels should be built around the kunai, so some gaps, enemy setups, or hazard layouts should be difficult or impossible without teleporting.

## 8. Art Style and World

The art style is 2D pixel steampunk with Arcane-style grime, Avatar-style scale, and readable platformer layouts so the world looks interesting while gameplay stays clear.

The world should include giant gears, smokestacks, bridges, rooftops, scrap-metal districts, glowing machines, dark metal, brass, teal energy, and flying whales in the background.

The environment should feel scrappy and handmade, like people built a city out of broken machines, old pipes, magic energy, and whatever metal they could find.

## 9. Sound and UI

Sound should include mechanical ambience, steam hissing, gear turning, metal clanks, wind, city noise, and energetic combat music.

The kunai throw and teleport need clear sound effects because the player should instantly feel when they throw, land, and teleport.

UI should stay simple with a main menu, health display, checkpoints, win screen, and lose screen, without adding too many extra menus.

## 10. Scope and Development Plan

Final scope should be realistic:

- 3 rooms
- 2 enemy types
- Optional boss
- Basic combat
- Kunai teleport
- Hazards
- Main menu
- Win screen
- Lose screen

First priority is getting movement and teleporting working. Then we will add combat and one test room. After the core mechanic feels good, we will build the rest of the levels.

We want to polish as much art as possible for world immersion.
